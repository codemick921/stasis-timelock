Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Stasis is a non-custodial time lock for USDC on Solana. This page describes what the app does with information, and it is written to match what the app actually does. If analytics are added later, this page changes first.

What the app collects

No accounts, no email address, no analytics, no names, no contacts, no location.

  • Your public wallet address, so the app can show your locks and build transactions. This is public information on Solana.
  • Your USDC and SOL balances, read from the chain.

The app stores two things in your browser: which wallet you selected, and a cached Mobile Wallet Adapter authorization so you do not re-approve the connection every time. Clearing site data removes both.

What goes to third parties

  • Solana RPC provider. Read requests and signed transactions. Like any web request, it can see your IP address and the accounts you query.
  • Hosting provider. Serves the app and keeps standard request logs.
  • The Solana blockchain. Approved transactions are public and permanent. Wallet addresses and lock amounts are visible on-chain.

The app has no server of its own that stores user data.

What the app never does

  • It never asks for or receives your seed phrase or private keys.
  • It never takes custody. USDC sits in a program-owned vault that only the program can release, and only to the wallet that funded it.
  • It does not sell or share data. There is no data to sell.

Your choices

Disconnect the wallet or clear site data to remove local state. You can host your own copy of the app and point it at your own RPC endpoint.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version is published here before the change ships.
