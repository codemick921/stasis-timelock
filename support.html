Support

Email support@YOURDOMAIN. We aim to reply within two business days.

Where did my USDC go?

It is in a program-owned vault, not in your wallet. The lock card shows the amount and unlock date, and links the vault address.

Can I get it back early?

No. There is no early exit, no cancel, and no admin override. That is the product.

Can you recover funds if I lose my wallet?

No. The vault releases only to the wallet that created the lock. If that key is lost, the funds stay locked forever.

My wallet says “Scam site detected”

Set the wallet’s network to Devnet. A wallet left on Mainnet blocks devnet transactions and labels the site as a scam.

I have no USDC

The app links to Circle’s devnet USDC faucet when your balance is zero.

Why does it cost SOL?

Creating a lock creates two on-chain accounts. Rent is about 0.003 SOL and is returned when you withdraw.

Is this audited?

Not yet. This is a devnet beta. Do not lock funds you cannot afford to leave alone.

Security issues

Email the address above with “security” in the subject. Please do not disclose publicly before we have had a chance to respond.
